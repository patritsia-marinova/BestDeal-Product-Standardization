# 04 – Data Exploration

## Overview

This folder contains the initial data exploration activities for our **BestDeal – AI Product Standardization** group project.

During this phase, our team began investigating the supplier datasets provided by BestDeal to understand their structure, available attributes, and potential challenges for product standardisation.

The main objective is to understand how product quantities and units of measurement are represented across different suppliers and determine how the available data could support an AI/ML solution.

This phase builds on the business analysis, requirements, and proposed workflows developed during the earlier stages of the project.

## Activities & Evidence

### 1. [Dataset Exploration](https://github.com/patritsia-marinova/BestDeal-Product-Standardization/blob/main/04%20%E2%80%93%20Data%20Exploration/dataset_exploration.html)

Our team started exploring the available supplier product data using a Jupyter Notebook.

The exploration supports our investigation into the following questions:

- How is product information structured across different supplier datasets?
- Which attributes are relevant for identifying product quantities and units?
- What inconsistencies or missing values could affect data standardisation?
- How can product information from different suppliers be prepared for comparison?
- What additional data preparation may be necessary before developing an AI/ML model?

This initial exploration is intended to help us better understand the datasets and identify potential challenges before selecting a suitable technical approach.

The notebook is exported as an HTML file to make the exploration accessible through the repository.

## Outcome

This phase represents the beginning of the technical investigation into BestDeal's supplier data.

The data exploration provides a starting point for understanding the available information and identifying what needs to be investigated further before developing a product standardisation solution.

As the project progresses, the team will continue working on data quality assessment, data preparation, and the investigation of suitable AI/ML approaches.

This phase will help connect the earlier **business analysis and solution design** activities with the practical development and evaluation of a technical solution.
