# BestDeal – AI Product Standardization

**Project Status: Work in Progress | Group Project**

## Project Overview

During Semester 4 of my ICT studies at Fontys University of Applied Sciences, specialising in **AI & Machine Learning**, I started working on a group project for **BestDeal**, a procurement platform offering approximately 1.2 million products from more than 50 suppliers.

The project focuses on improving the consistency of supplier product information, particularly **product quantities and units of measurement**.

Currently, suppliers provide product data in different formats, making it difficult to compare equivalent products and prices accurately. Our goal is to develop an **AI-supported solution** that standardises product information while preserving the original supplier ordering requirements.

The project combines **business process analysis, stakeholder communication, data analysis, and AI development** to explore opportunities for improving product data management.

## Project Objectives

1. Understand BestDeal's current supplier data management process.
2. Identify bottlenecks caused by inconsistent product quantities and measurement units.
3. Analyse the existing workflow and identify opportunities for automation.
4. Design an improved process that standardises product information while retaining supplier-specific requirements.
5. Explore an AI-based approach to automate product data standardisation.
6. Evaluate the proposed solution against BestDeal's business needs.

## Project Approach & Progress

### 1. Project Initiation & Stakeholder Communication

Our team started by reviewing the project case, establishing group agreements, and preparing questions for the first client meeting.

During the meeting, we gathered information about BestDeal's current processes, challenges, and expectations. Afterwards, we documented the findings in meeting notes and a debrief.

We also developed a project plan outlining the team's activities, responsibilities, and objectives.

### 2. Business Process Analysis

Based on the information gathered, we analysed BestDeal's current workflow and developed an **IST process diagram**.

This helped us identify bottlenecks related to inconsistent supplier units, product quantities, and manual processing activities.

The findings provide a foundation for designing an improved **SOLL process**, identifying gaps between the current and desired situations, and exploring opportunities for AI-supported automation.

### 3. AI Solution Development

The next stage focuses on investigating how AI can support the standardisation of supplier product data.

The intended solution should recognise and standardise different quantities and units of measurement, making product comparisons more consistent without changing the original supplier ordering information.

This stage is still under development.
