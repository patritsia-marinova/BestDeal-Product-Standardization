# BestDeal – AI Product Standardization

**Project Status: Work in Progress | Group Project**

## Project Overview

During Semester 4 of my ICT studies at Fontys University of Applied Sciences, specialising in **AI & Machine Learning**, I am working on a group project for **BestDeal**, a procurement platform offering approximately 1.2 million products from more than 50 suppliers.

The project focuses on improving the consistency of supplier product information, particularly **product quantities and units of measurement**.

Currently, suppliers provide product data in different formats, making it difficult to compare equivalent products and their prices accurately. Our goal is to investigate these inconsistencies and develop an **AI-supported standardisation solution** that makes product comparisons more reliable while preserving the original supplier ordering requirements.

The project combines **business process analysis, stakeholder communication, PIM system research, data exploration, and machine learning** to investigate how product data standardisation can be improved.

## Project Objectives

1. Understand BestDeal's current supplier data management process.
2. Identify bottlenecks related to inconsistent product quantities and measurement units.
3. Define business and technical requirements for product standardisation.
4. Research existing Product Information Management (PIM) systems and potential integration approaches.
5. Design an improved workflow using AI-supported product mapping and standardisation.
6. Explore supplier datasets and investigate suitable technical approaches.
7. Develop and evaluate a solution that supports accurate product and price comparisons.

## Project Approach & Progress

### 1. Project Initiation & Stakeholder Research

Our team started by reviewing the project case, establishing group agreements, and preparing questions for the client meetings.

Through stakeholder communication, meeting notes, and debriefing, we gathered information about BestDeal's current processes, challenges, and expectations.

We also developed a project plan to organise the team's responsibilities, activities, and expected deliverables.

### 2. Business Analysis & Requirements

We analysed the existing supplier data workflow and developed an **IST process diagram**, identifying bottlenecks related to manual product mapping and inconsistent quantities and units.

Based on the business problem and stakeholder information, we investigated the requirements for the proposed solution and prioritised them using the **MoSCoW method**.

An important requirement is to standardise product information for comparison purposes while keeping the original supplier ordering details available in the backend.

### 3. PIM Research & Solution Design

Our team researched existing Product Information Management systems, including **Akeneo and Pimcore**, to understand how product data is imported, mapped, transformed, and stored.

The research helped us identify potential approaches for standardising supplier quantities and units before the information enters BestDeal's PIM.

We also designed proposed workflows illustrating how an AI-supported solution could:

- Identify and map equivalent product information from different suppliers.
- Standardise quantities and measurement units.
- Calculate comparable prices using standardised measurements.
- Include human validation and manual handling when necessary.
- Store standardised information in the PIM while preserving supplier-specific ordering requirements.

These workflows represent proposed solutions, and their technical feasibility and integration with BestDeal's existing systems still require validation.

### 4. Data Exploration & AI Development

The team has started exploring the available product data to better understand its structure and suitability for standardisation.

The next stages involve further data analysis, investigating appropriate AI/ML approaches, and developing and evaluating a prototype.

**This stage is ongoing, and no final AI solution has been completed yet.**
